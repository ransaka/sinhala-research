# Phase 1 status

The research scope is a controlled Sinhala CTC comparison of Unicode code points, SentencePiece, and sinlib phonological units with one fixed pretrained speech encoder. The sinlib advantage remains a hypothesis. CharBERT correction is optional if time permits.

The researcher reports supervisor acceptance of this scope, OpenSLR use, and the corpus audit. The voluntary contribution site is handled separately by the researcher. No site code, recordings, corpus files, model weights, or personal data are in this repository.

The final speaker split and corpus audit artifacts are kept outside Git. A local provisional speaker-grouped manifest is available for exploratory pipeline work; it is not a final benchmark manifest.
